Weekend — three voices, one passage

Same text, three speakers. Every contour is drawn in semitones relative to that speaker's own median, so the shape of the melody is directly comparable even though the voices sit far apart in absolute pitch. Click any contour to play from that spot. ● record yourself on any row — your contour is drawn dashed on top, then ▶ mine replays you and ▶ A/B plays the speaker then you. Both the passage text and the voices are AI-generated.
Unit: semitones (st) vs each speaker's median (0 st line). Same-x scale within a sentence, so longer bars = slower pacing. Word pitch = mean F0 over the word. Gray bands = silent pauses. Overlay panel is time-normalized (0–100 % of each speaker's sentence) to compare melody shape only.
