Targeted training, one skill at a time. Blue = target voice (semitones vs the speaker's median; shaded area = loudness). ▶ play · ⟳ loop · 0.75× slow · ● record yourself — your contour is drawn dashed on top. Both the sentences and the voice are AI-generated.

Overview — pick a skill

Semitones are relative to the speaker's overall median (0 line), the same reference for every clip. Your recorded contour is plotted against your own median, so only the shape is compared. Floor line = this clip's 5th percentile; ceiling guide = +4 st.
The sentence text itself is drawn as a melody — each word colored (blue = low, red = high) and raised or lowered by its pitch vs the speaker's median; after you record, a second "you" line renders your melody on the same words for side-by-side comparison (your colors are relative to your own median, so only the shape is compared). Longer sentences show a thought groups row — pick a group and play / loop / record work on just that span (the shaded region); "all" returns to the full sentence. Recordings become takes under each card — record as many as you like and click a chip to switch between them. ★ keeps a take in this browser (on this device) across reloads; ☁ publishes it onto this site so anyone opening the page — e.g. your coach — can hear it (public; one-time token setup); ⬇ downloads it as an audio file; unstarred takes vanish when you leave the page. Dashed chips are published takes.
